## Immutable target and later evidence

Freeze the implementation/package/documentation candidate commit before building the final wheel and distribution archive. The reviewed source SHA and exact payload hashes identify the release target. Subsequent tracked inventory, draft/public readback receipts, summaries and GSD administrative closure necessarily occur in later commits on the same branch; they are evidence about that immutable target, not changes to it. They are linked from the release notes and final main branch. Never require a receipt to contain its own commit or hash, and never retarget a published tag. Before publication require unchanged code/resources/wheel/payload hashes and accepted CI for the candidate implementation. Any payload change requires a new candidate audit and affected verification; documentation-only evidence commits do not silently replace the tagged payload.

Existing SCOPE, PRD, ARCHITECTURE, METHODOLOGY, OPERATIONS, SOURCES, GLOSSARY, CONTRIBUTING, docs/README and CI workflow are read-only verification inputs for this plan, not planned edits.
