<domain>

</domain>

<decisions>
- One validated v2 public projection and canonical evidence-bound findings feed every format. Rendering never receives diagnostic estimates. Suppression applies to prose, reasons, alternative text, tables, figure labels, PDF and CSV/Parquet/DuckDB alike.
- Organize by reader questions: up to three supported opening findings, national context, the three field profiles, quarterly evolution, territory and recorded sex, coverage, limitations, methods and exact sources. Show unsupported cells and why they are unavailable. Do not substitute a JSON dump for editorial interpretation.
- Use a coherent print-oriented visual system, legible Spanish typography, restrained colors with sufficient contrast, clear units and uncertainty, and tables accompanying figures. No network fonts, scripts or analytics. All assets require license review.
- Verify print pagination and accents, chart/table equivalence, mobile-width HTML and 200% zoom. The existing WeasyPrint 70 Windows proof validates the toolchain only; final PDF visual inspection and Linux rendering remain required.
- Preserve the v1 synthetic fixture workflow as regression coverage. Make the real research workflow unambiguous in the CLI and documentation, with explicit source/output roots and offline replay.
- Resolve every required current acquisition before building or opening the current publication. A later failed acquisition invalidates dependent current access even if an older sealed report remains on disk. Historical immutable runs remain available as history, never as a fresh result.
- Seal all output hashes and source/method/claim references before atomic current promotion. A failed acquisition, estimation, validation, render, export or seal operation leaves a failure receipt and no valid current publication. Do not overwrite historical bundles.
- Replaying identical approved snapshots must reproduce numerical content and canonical public records. Run IDs and timestamps may differ and must be identified separately; do not pretend PDF metadata or acquisition timestamps are numerically substantive.
- Publish documented stable join keys derived from the v2 grain (which has no record.id), units, null/status meanings and provenance. Keep dimensions separate in exports and do not infer zeros.
- The release bundle contains aggregates, figures, reports, manifests and acceptance evidence. Raw ZIPs, person-derived frames, local software/cache files and credentials remain excluded.
</decisions>
